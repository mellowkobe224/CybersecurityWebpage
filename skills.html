01 / TECHNICAL SKILLS

Building Cybersecurity Skills

Cybersecurity professionals need a strong understanding of computers and networks. Learning how computers communicate can make it easier to identify weaknesses and understand how attacks can affect an organization.

Programming is another valuable skill for cybersecurity. Languages such as Python, Java, JavaScript, and C can help security professionals automate tasks, analyze information, and understand how software works.

Problem solving and critical thinking are also important skills. Cybersecurity professionals must be able to investigate unusual activity, identify potential vulnerabilities, and determine how to protect systems from future attacks.

Programming

Programming can help security professionals understand software and automate tasks.

Networking

Understanding networks helps professionals identify weaknesses and protect systems.
