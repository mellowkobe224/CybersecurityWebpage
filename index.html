01 / CYBERSECURITY

Welcome to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and information from digital attacks. As technology becomes an important part of everyday life, cybersecurity professionals help organizations protect their systems and data.

A career in cybersecurity can involve many different areas of technology. Security professionals may investigate suspicious activity, protect networks, test applications for vulnerabilities, or help organizations respond to cyberattacks.

My interest in cybersecurity comes from wanting to understand how technology works and how it can be protected. Learning programming, networking, and security concepts can provide a strong foundation for starting a career in the cybersecurity field.
