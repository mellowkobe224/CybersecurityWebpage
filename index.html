Why Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity interests me because technology is constantly changing and there are always new problems to solve. I enjoy learning how computers, networks, and software work and understanding how they can be protected from people who may try to misuse them.

One of the things I enjoy most about cybersecurity is the investigative side of the field. Security professionals have to look at information, recognize unusual behavior, and determine what could be happening behind the scenes. I like the idea of using technology and problem-solving skills to investigate these types of situations.

Cybersecurity also stands out to me because it requires continuous learning. New vulnerabilities, attacks, and technologies are constantly being introduced. This makes cybersecurity a career where I can continue developing my knowledge instead of doing the exact same thing every day.

What I Enjoy About Cybersecurity

Problem Solving

I enjoy working through difficult technical problems and figuring out how different pieces of a system work together.

Technology

Cybersecurity gives me the opportunity to work with computers, networks, programming, and other areas of technology that interest me.

Investigation

I enjoy the idea of investigating suspicious activity, finding vulnerabilities, and understanding how security incidents happen.
