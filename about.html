My Interest in Cybersecurity

I created this website to explain why cybersecurity is a career field that interests me. I have always been interested in technology, and cybersecurity gives me a way to combine that interest with problem solving and critical thinking.

What I enjoy about cybersecurity is that there is always something new to learn. Instead of technology staying the same, new software, vulnerabilities, attacks, and security techniques continue to develop. This makes the field challenging and gives me opportunities to keep improving.

My goal is to continue developing my computer science and cybersecurity skills so that I can eventually enter the cybersecurity industry. I want to gain hands-on experience, learn from real-world situations, and build the knowledge necessary to become a successful cybersecurity professional.

Why I Created This Website

This website gives me an opportunity to share my interest in cybersecurity while also practicing the web development skills I am learning.

The different pages explain cybersecurity, the skills that interest me, and the career paths I may want to pursue.

As I continue learning, I hope to expand my knowledge and use future projects to demonstrate the technical skills I develop.
